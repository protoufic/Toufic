Clear answers

What a serious partner should know.

Is the Guinness World Records title confirmed?

No. This is a world-record attempt. Application, final guidelines, qualifying requirements and verification remain subject to confirmation.

Are all six races selected?

No. Each race is announced only after rules, entry, visa, training, recovery, travel, funding, academic commitments and medical and coaching approval are controlled.

Can a brand support one part instead of the entire mission?

Yes. A partner can lead the mission, back one continent, provide one operational category, or support a specific function.

What can a partner receive?

Depending on the agreement: campaign position, content, product use, employee activity, customer activation, speaking, media material, category rights and reporting.

What happens if a race date changes?

The mission plan adapts. Deliverables are tied to the campaign and approved race chapters, with changes documented transparently.

Can the proposal be customized?

Yes. The website qualification flow starts with the company goal so the proposal can be built around the right outcome.
