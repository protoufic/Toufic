The Opportunity

What is happening. Why it matters. Why now.

Toufic Abou Ali, a 20-year-old Lebanese founder-athlete, is attempting to become the youngest person to complete six full IRONMAN races on six continents before November 27, 2027.

Each IRONMAN is 226 km — a 3.8 km swim, 180 km bike, and 42.2 km run. Six races means 1,356 km of documented racing across the globe.

This is rare. It has a clear story. It has proof. It has a deadline. It has a Lebanese founder behind it who builds companies, manages teams, and operates at an unusual level of discipline.

The brand that partners early owns the origin of the story. Later partners can join chapters, but they cannot own the beginning.
