200-Word Bio

Toufic Abou Ali is a 20-year-old Lebanese founder-athlete based in Lebanon. He is the Founder & CEO of Sira, a career platform that serves a community of over 60,000 people and operates with a team of more than 25.

Toufic is attempting to become the youngest person to complete six full IRONMAN races — each consisting of a 3.8 km swim, 180 km bike, and 42.2 km run — across six continents before his deadline of November 27, 2027. The total distance: 1,356 km. The current public benchmark for the youngest completion is 21 years and 167 days.

He finished his first IRONMAN 70.3 in Warsaw, Poland, on June 7, 2026, in 6:08:15, at age 19 — seven days before turning 20. The race exposed preparation gaps that became the foundation of his Six Continents campaign.

Before turning 20, Toufic had completed two marathons, five official half marathons (with a personal best of 1:29:26), earned a 1st U18 podium at the ISF Half Marathon, and recorded a 10 km personal best of 39:42.

Toufic studies abroad, manages a team, and carries Lebanon into every race. The Guinness World Records application and final rules remain subject to confirmation. No record or race finish is guaranteed.
