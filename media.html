100-Word Bio

Toufic Abou Ali is a 20-year-old Lebanese founder-athlete and Co-Founder & Co-CEO of Sira, a career platform with a 60,000-person community and 25+ person team. He is attempting to become the youngest person to complete six full IRONMAN races across six continents with a working deadline of November 27, 2027. He finished his first IRONMAN 70.3 in Warsaw in 6:08:15 at age 19. Before turning 20, he had completed 50 documented races across 4 countries with 28 podiums, two marathons, five half marathons, and was featured on MTV Lebanon for running 10K on a broken leg.
