## GoFundMe

### France organizer route

France is a supported organizer country. The person receiving withdrawals must satisfy the requirements for the country where the fundraiser is created. For France and supported European Union withdrawals, GoFundMe lists an eligible bank account, a supported phone number, valid identification, an address in the signup country, and a minimum age of 18.

The public fundraiser currency is EUR. A supporter pays in the fundraiser currency and their bank may apply foreign exchange charges. GoFundMe says it cannot process donations made from Lebanon, which is why the website offers Lebanese wallets separately.

For a fundraiser created in France, GoFundMe publishes no setup or management fee and a transaction fee of 2.9 percent plus EUR 0.25 per donation. Optional donor tips are separate.

The mission cash target supplied by Toufic is EUR 30,000. GoFundMe may show a smaller automatic milestone. The website keeps EUR 30,000 as the mission target and does not describe an automatic milestone as the total budget.

### Public organizer disclosure

Toufic Abou Ali organizes this fundraiser and receives withdrawals through his verified French banking route. Funds are used for the documented costs of the Six Continents mission. Public progress counts cash actually received. Commitments and in kind support are reported separately.

## Whish

Whish publishes USD and LBP wallet support, wallet transfers, payment links and Whish Me QR transfers. It publishes Whish to Whish transfers and Whish Me as free, a 3 percent payment link commission, a 3 percent international card commission, and 1 percent USD cash out. The live app controls the actual fee.

Toufic's current public route is not a payment link. It is a wallet transfer to `76 923 943` after the visitor selects Whish.

Official sources:

3. [Fees and commissions](https://www.whish.money/fees-commissions)

## OMT Pay

OMT Pay publishes USD and LBP wallet balances, free wallet to wallet transfers, local or international card funding, and QR payment for participating merchants. Public provider material does not establish one universal fee for every personal receiving route, so the website instructs supporters to review the fee in the app.

Toufic's current public route is not a payment link. It is a wallet transfer to `76 923 943` after the visitor selects OMT Pay.

Official source: [OMT Pay](https://www.omt.com.lb/en/omt-pay)

## Purpl

Purpl's terms say a user can send money to another Purpl wallet or any Lebanese mobile number. If the recipient is not registered, they must register and claim the transfer; an unclaimed transfer is returned after 14 days. Purpl also advertises payment links for merchants and service providers and top ups by local or international cards. Fees vary and are shown before confirmation.

Toufic's current website route uses the confirmed mobile number, not a Purpl merchant payment link.

Official sources:

1. [Purpl](https://www.purplwallet.com/)
2. [Purpl terms and conditions](https://purplme.com/terms-conditions/)

## MyMonty

MyMonty says users can send money by mobile number, transfer link or QR and can request money using a QR or link. Its fee schedule effective 4 September 2026 lists sending to another MyMonty wallet and receiving as free. It lists card funding through a payment link at 3 percent for local or international cards. The live review screen remains controlling.

Toufic's current website route uses the confirmed mobile number. A direct request link or QR can replace the manual route later if Toufic generates and tests one.

Official sources:

1. [MyMonty transfers](https://mymonty.com.lb/transfers)
2. [MyMonty fees and charges](https://mymonty.com.lb/fees-and-charges/List-of-Fees-and-Charges.pdf)
