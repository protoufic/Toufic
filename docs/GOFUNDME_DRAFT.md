### How is progress calculated?

The public meter counts cash received toward the EUR 30,000 mission target. Committed support and in kind help appear separately and are not counted as cash.

Yes. `toufic.co/support` provides Whish, OMT Pay, Purpl and MyMonty instructions after the visitor selects Lebanon.
