Understand the scale

One IRONMAN is not one marathon.

It contains a full marathon — after the swim and bike. That is why the order matters.

Swim 3.8 km. Cycle 180 km. Then run 42.2 km. The clock does not stop between them.

Six races multiply every part of that demand: training, recovery, travel, visas, equipment, medical support, race proof and execution.
