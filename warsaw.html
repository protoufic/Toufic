What the race proved

Toufic could finish. The race showed exactly what had to change.

The goal was sub-five hours. The result was 6:08:15.

The bike leg exposed the largest gap: almost no outdoor riding experience, first clipless race, first aero-bike race setup, falls, low hydration and an untested fueling plan.

The run still finished in 1:51:56 without walking. Toughness was present. Professional execution was not yet complete.

Firsts

First open-water race, wetsuit race, triathlon bike race, clipless race, transitions and half marathon after a 90 km bike.

Race-day gaps

Bike handling, race simulation, open-water preparation, brick training, fueling and hydration.
