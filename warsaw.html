The honest version

Finished. But not prepared well enough.

The result was not the goal. It was the audit that created the Six Continents response.

Almost no outdoor cyclingLimited technical control and confidence.
No open-water race preparationThe first race swim was also the first open-water race.
No bricks or full simulationThe body and system were never rehearsed together.
Weak hydration and fuelingAbout 250 ml consumed over the 90 km bike.
The response

Toughness can finish a race.
Preparation controls the result.

Six Continents must be built professionally, not survived through improvisation.

01

Build the athlete

Professional coaching, technical cycling, open water, bricks and complete simulations.

02

Control race day

Tested fueling, hydration, equipment, transitions, medical support and recovery.

03

Prove every chapter

Official results, GPS, photography, video, witnesses and partner reporting.
