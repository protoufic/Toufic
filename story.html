The short story

Toufic grew up between two homes after his parents divorced at birth. At 13, overweight and bullied, he started training and working on himself. Sport became the first place he learned discipline. Business became the second.

He started businesses at 13, failed repeatedly, worked remotely for a US company at 17, co-founded Sira before 18, moved alone to France, completed two marathons and finished IRONMAN 70.3 Warsaw before turning 20.
