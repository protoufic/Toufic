The founder story

A record attempt needs an operator, not only an athlete.

The Six Continents mission combines training, funding, travel, evidence, university, health, media, partnerships and race execution.

Toufic started building businesses as a teenager, later worked in remote creative operations, and founded Sira before turning 18.

He moved alone to France, studies management, leads Sira across borders, and competes in endurance sport. The mission does not remove those responsibilities. It raises the standard required to manage them.
