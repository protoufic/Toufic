The Mission

Six Continents.
Six Full IRONMAN Races.
One World-Record Attempt.

This is not six random races. It is one global campaign told through six chapters.

1,356 km across the world.

Lebanese founder-athlete Toufic Abou Ali is preparing to complete six full IRONMAN races — each consisting of a 3.8 km swim, 180 km bike, and 42.2 km run — across six continents before his deadline of November 27, 2027.

One full IRONMAN is 226 km. Six full IRONMAN races equal 1,356 km of racing. The current public benchmark for youngest completion is 21 years and 167 days. Toufic is 20.

The Record

The target is not one finish line.

The current public benchmark for the youngest person to complete six full IRONMAN races on six continents is 21 years and 167 days. Toufic is currently 20.

The Guinness World Records application and final rules remain subject to confirmation. The working deadline is November 27, 2027.

This is an independent campaign. No record or race finish is guaranteed.

Working Deadline

Why Lebanon matters.

Lebanon is not a backdrop. It is the center of this story. A 20-year-old Lebanese founder-athlete attempting a world record that has never been attempted from Lebanon. Every race carries Lebanon onto a global stage.

The mission is athletic. It is also cultural, commercial, and national. Lebanon is the origin of the story, and it is the reason the story is rare.

Campaign Structure

Each continent is a chapter.

Each race is not a standalone event. It is a chapter in a larger campaign with a repeatable, professional structure.

1. Preparation

Training plan, location research, course analysis, equipment review, and partner coordination.

2. Travel

The journey to the race location. Documented content from arrival through race week.

3. Race Week

Course preview, registration, equipment check, partner activation, and race preparation.

4. Race Day

Swim. Bike. Run. The full race documented from start to finish.

5. Result

Official time, placement, split analysis, and honest assessment.

6. Lesson & Report

What worked. What did not. What changes. Deliverable report for partners.

Founding Position

Why early partners receive more value.

The founding position is only available before the campaign is fully established. Early partners help shape the platform, define the content, and own the origin story of this mission.

Later partners will be able to join specific chapters. But they will not be able to own the origin. They will not be present from the beginning. They will not have shaped the campaign.

Deliverables and rights are agreed clearly. There is no fake limited timer. There is no false scarcity. The founding position is simply a real advantage that disappears as the campaign grows.
