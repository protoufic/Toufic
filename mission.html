How the mission gets executed

No improvisation.
Six controlled systems.

Warsaw showed the cost of relying on toughness. The full mission must be built around preparation and proof.

01

Professional coaching

Long-term training, technical cycling, open water, bricks and complete simulations.

02

Medical control

Testing, physiotherapy, recovery and race spacing based on the athlete, not the calendar alone.

03

Race selection

Only races that satisfy final rules, entry, visa, university and recovery constraints.

04

Travel and equipment

Flights, bike transport, accommodation, local movement and backup plans.

05

Fueling and hydration

Tested systems for training, travel, race day and recovery.

06

Evidence and reporting

Official results, GPS, photography, video, witnesses, backups and partner reports.
